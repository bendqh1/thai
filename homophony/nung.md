| Thai | Phonetic | Meaning | Notes |
|------|----------|---------|-------|
| นึง OR หนึ่ง | nung\\ OR hnung\\ | one | the literal form of the number 1 |
| นึ่ง | nung✓ | steam | cooking method |

## Notes

* These words may remind of the word `งา` for `sesame`.
* These words may remind of the word `องุ่น` for `grapes`.
* These words may remind of the word `หงุดหงิด` for `disapointed`.
