| Thai | Phonetic | Meaning | Notes |
|------|----------|---------|-------|
| ใกล้ | glla^ii | near | long rising-falling sound |
| ไกล | glaii | far | short rising sound |

## Notes

Some people would just say `X glai glai gab Y` and so doubling any glai would just mean "near" like `X is near with Y` but this doubling thing is not really a solution for proper expression of the difference in vocal andor written language.
