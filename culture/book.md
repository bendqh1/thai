## Physical depth of bowing

| Thai Term    | English Description                          |
|--------------|----------------------------------------------|
| ก้ม          | Simple lowering of the head                  |
| ไหว้         | Palms pressed together with a bow            |
| คำนับ        | Formal half bow used in various ocassions        |
| หมอบคลาน     | Kneeling-crawling posture in very formal rites (close to the king) |
| กราบ         | Full prostration to the ground               |

เคารพ can be either of these.
