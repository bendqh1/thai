The main religious buildings in Thailand include the following.

## 1. Ancient / Pan-Buddhist Structures

These are structures or architectural concepts with **ancient Buddhist precedents**, rather than specifically Thai forms.

| คำศัพท์ | Type | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| สถูป | Sacred monument | One of the oldest distinctive forms of Buddhist sacred architecture, originating in the Indian Buddhist world. |
| เจดีย์ | Sacred monument | The Thai term associated with the Buddhist stupa/reliquary tradition. The underlying concept is ancient, although the Thai architectural form is much later. |
| วิหาร | Religious / monastic building | Buddhist halls and monastic buildings have ancient Indian precedents. The specific Thai viharn form developed much later. |
| กุฏิ | Monastic building | Residential quarters for monks have ancient Buddhist precedents. The specific Thai kuti form is later. |
| วัด | Monastic establishment | Buddhist monasteries and monastic compounds are ancient, although the recognizable Thai wat complex is a later Southeast Asian development. |

## 2. Later / Specifically Thai Buddhist Architecture

These are features that are strongly associated with **Thai Buddhist temples**, particularly mainstream Thai Buddhism, but should not be projected backward into the earliest Buddhist period.

| คำศัพท์ | Type | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| วัด | Thai Buddhist temple complex | The standard institutional and architectural setting of mainstream Thai Buddhism. |
| วัดใหญ่ | Large temple complex | A descriptive term for a large or particularly important wat, rather than a separate ancient architectural type. |
| เจดีย์ใหญ่ | Large chedi | A large-scale Thai Buddhist chedi; the adjective describes its size rather than creating a separate architectural category. |
| ปรางค์ | Religious tower | A later South/Southeast Asian architectural form, particularly associated with Khmer and Thai architecture. |
| วิหาร | Temple hall | The specifically Thai form of the Buddhist religious hall, used for worship, ceremonies, preaching, and Buddha images. |
| กุฏิ | Monk's residence | The Thai form of the monastic residence within a wat. |
| บ่อน้ำอธิษฐาน | Sacred feature | A devotional or wishing feature that may occur at Thai Buddhist sites, but is not a fundamental Buddhist architectural form. |
| พระบิณฑบาตร | Religious object / representation | Associated with monks receiving alms; not a building type. |

## 3. Buddhist Temple Iconography, Guardians & Etiquette

This category separates **objects, figures, signs, and behavioral rules** from actual buildings. Some of these traditions are shared with Hinduism and Thai folk religion rather than being exclusively Buddhist.

| คำศัพท์ | Type | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| พญานาค | Sacred / guardian figure | A serpent-like figure frequently used at Thai Buddhist temples, especially on stairways and entrances. The Nāga tradition predates and extends beyond Thai Buddhism. |
| ยักษ์ | Guardian figure | Mythological temple guardians, particularly common at entrances. The tradition has connections with wider South and Southeast Asian religious mythology. |
| สิงห์ | Guardian figure | Lion-like guardian figures used around Thai Buddhist temples and other sacred sites. |
| สำรวมกาย — วาจาใจ | Religious principle | Maintaining restraint and discipline in body, speech, and mind. |
| รักษาความสงบ | Temple etiquette | Maintaining peace and quiet within the religious environment. |
| ป้าย “ห้ามนั่ง” | Sign | Prohibits sitting in a designated area. |
| ป้าย “ห้ามสูบบุหรี่” | Sign | Prohibits smoking. |
| ป้าย “ห้ามเล่นการพนัน” | Sign | Prohibits gambling. |
| ป้าย “ห้ามดื่มเหล้า” | Sign | Prohibits drinking alcohol. |
| ป้าย “ห้ามพิง” | Sign | Prohibits leaning against designated structures or sacred objects. |
| ป้าย “ห้ามปีนป่าย” | Sign | Prohibits climbing on structures or objects. |
| ป้าย “ห้ามเหยียบธรณีประตู” | Sign | Prohibits stepping directly on a doorway threshold. |
| ป้าย “ห้ามกอดพระพุทธรูป” | Sign | Prohibits hugging Buddha images. |
| ป้าย “ห้ามนั่งหันเท้าไปทางพระพุทธรูป” | Sign | Prohibits sitting with one's feet pointing toward a Buddha image. |
| ป้าย “ปิดมือถือหรือปิดเสียง” | Sign | Requests visitors to turn off or silence mobile phones. |

## 4. Thai Folk Religion & Syncretic Sacred Structures

This category is for the **Thai folk-religious layer**, particularly the syncretic environment combining Thai folk religion, Chinese/Shenist traditions, Hinduism, and Buddhism.

| คำศัพท์ | Type | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| ศาลพระภูมิ | Spirit shrine | A small shrine associated with the guardian spirit of a property or location. |
| ศาลพระใหญ่ | Large spirit shrine | A larger shrine dedicated to a locally venerated spirit or sacred figure. |
| ศาลเจ้า | Shrine / religious building | A larger shrine particularly associated with Chinese folk-religious traditions, often existing within a broader syncretic religious environment. |
| พญานาค | Sacred / guardian figure | Can function within the broader syncretic religious landscape, although it is also strongly associated with Buddhism. |
| ยักษ์ | Sacred / guardian figure | Can function within overlapping Buddhist, Hindu, and folk-religious traditions. |
| สิงห์ | Sacred / guardian figure | Guardian imagery that can occur across Buddhist, Hindu, Chinese, and local Southeast Asian sacred environments. |
| บ่อน้ำอธิษฐาน | Sacred feature | A place associated with wishing, prayer, or popular devotional practice; not uniquely Buddhist. |
